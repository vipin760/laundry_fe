How to request account deletion

Account deletion is self-service and happens inside the LaundryBrew app. To delete your account:

  1. Open the LaundryBrew app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account.
  3. Review what will be deleted and what will be retained, then confirm.
  4. Verify your identity (password, OTP, or your Google/Apple sign-in) to prevent unauthorized deletion.
  5. Confirm one final time. Your account is deleted and you're signed out of every device automatically.

Don't have access to the app, or need help? Email support@laundrybrew.com from your registered email or phone number and we'll process the deletion for you.

What gets deleted vs. retained

When your deletion request is confirmed, we immediately deactivate and sign you out of your account. Personal data is permanently deleted or anonymised; a small set of records is kept only where the law requires it.

Deleted

  • Name, email, phone number
  • Profile photo
  • Saved addresses
  • Saved preferences
  • Notification settings & device tokens
  • Referral information
  • Login sessions (all devices)
  • Wallet balance (if ₹0; otherwise see below)

Retained (legally required)

  • Completed order history
  • Payment & transaction records
  • GST invoices
  • Tax records
  • Fraud-prevention & audit logs

Retained records are kept linked to an anonymised account (your name, contact details, and other personal identifiers are removed) — they are never used to identify you again, only to satisfy tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention obligations.

Retention period: up to 30 days after your request, then permanently anonymised. Legally required financial/tax records (orders, invoices, payment records) are kept for the statutory period required under Indian tax law.

Non-zero wallet balance

If you have a remaining wallet balance, contact support@laundrybrew.com before deleting your account to arrange a refund — wallet balances cannot be recovered once the account is deleted.

Questions

For anything about this process, write to support@laundrybrew.com. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
